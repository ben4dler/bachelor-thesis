\section{Pipeline}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item EEG in ein Array laden (T, 64), T: Anzahl der Zeitpunkte (also die Zeit die vergangen ist in Sekunden oder ms), N: Anzahl der Kanäle/Neuronen (in unserem Fall 64)
    \item Die Kanäle für alle Personen in derselben Reihenfolge herausfiltern
    \item Die Eventmarke auf eine Label-Zeitreihe B abbilden
    \item Die Eventklassen auf 0 bis 7 reduzieren oder die letzte Dense-Schicht anpassen
    \item X und B zeitlich exakt synchronisieren (also Neuronen aktivität mit Label)
    \item Danach prep\_data(X, B, win=15) aufrufen
\end{enumerate}

Bundle Net Continous anschauen, ob es besser geeignet ist für EEG Daten \\


Für BunDLe-Net gilt daher:

\begin{itemize}
    \item X = EEG-Signal über die Zeit, also Kanäle × Samples.
    \item Die Events sind zunächst nur zeitlich punktuelle Marker.
    \item B kann daraus abgeleitet werden, wenn du EEG-Epochen klassifizieren möchtest.
    \item Die Events sind weder 'Inaktivität' noch direkt kontinuierliche Zuständ
\end{itemize}

Eine sinnvolle Gruppierung für state\_names wäre beispielsweise:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Fixation / kein Stimulus
    \item Gesicht
    \item Tier
    \item Ort
    \item Objekt
    \item Catch-Trial
    \item Button-Press
\end{itemize}

\section{Problems}
The architecture of BunDLe-Net is way to badly optimized for this kind of data load. For just one subject the Computer would need storage of 60 GiB to store one array. 
In order to fix this, I will reduce from float64 to float32. Also I will need to introduce a window mechanism, in order to sequentially send one part of the array to model.tau. 

Notes on the subjects of the study: 
\begin{itemize}
    \item sub-101 has a break in recording after some time. Need to zip the two parts together without the break in the middle.
    \item sub-102 has a normal recording. 
    \item sub-103 has a kind of small break from approx. 2500 to 2600 seconds. 
    
    
\end{itemize}

\section{Dataset}
The dataset comes from the study "Healthy aging delays and dedifferentiates high-level
visual representations". The set consists of 43 individuals, 21 young (19 - 33 years of age)
and 22 older subjects (60 - 73 years of age). No participant had to be left out for the EEG dataset.
The dataset consists of different trials corresponding to different image categories. 
There are in total 4 different image categories grouped into two supra categories. 
The categories are faces, animals, objects, places grouped into animate and inanimate respectefly. 
There are in total 64 images which are each shown 32 times. So in total that amounts to 2048 trials. 
Cath trials are made every 4-6 trials, showing an image of a smurf. The participants are instructed to 
press a button and blink to complete this trial. 

\section{Preprocessing}
The study already implements a preprocessing pipeline which consits of a notch filter and a baseline
correction. 
In the open source code, which was used to produce the results used in the study, I could find some more 
preprocessing being done. These steps are as follows: Rejection and interpolation of bad channels, subsampling
to 200 Hz and MVNN.
